Three checks that expose a fake FLAC

Most “lossless” downloads are not lossless. These take under a minute:

  • Do the size math. A 4-minute track at CD quality is ~25–35 MB; at 24-bit/96 kHz, 60–90 MB; a 320 kbps MP3 is 9–11 MB. An 8 MB “lossless FLAC” of a 4-minute song is an MP3 in a FLAC container.
  • Read the bitrate, not the extension. Real FLAC reports ~700–1100 kbps at CD quality. A file labelled FLAC that reports 320 or 128 kbps was re-wrapped.
  • Check the spectrum. Genuine lossless extends past 20 kHz with natural roll-off; a 128 kbps MP3 converted to FLAC cuts hard around 16 kHz.
